## Created/Modified Files

### 1. Pipeline Progress Tracker

**Created: `training/pipeline_progress_tracker.py`**

- Tracks 5 pipeline stages: data_loading, ssl_pretrain, waypoint_bc, rl_refinement, carla_eval
- StageStatus: captures enabled, checkpoints, latest_run, latest_metrics
- PipelineStatus: overall health (no_data/initializing/partial/healthy), next_stage, blockers
- Stage checkers: look for checkpoints in out/{ssl,bc,sft,rl,eval}/ directories
- Loads metrics.json from latest runs (loss, reward, ADE, FDE)
- Computes next_stage: first stage without checkpoints
- CLI: --json, --output, --workspace, --stages

**Smoke test**: ✅ PASSED
- data_loading: enabled=true, data_available=true
- ssl_pretrain: enabled=false
- waypoint_bc: enabled=false
- rl_refinement: enabled=false
- carla_eval: enabled=true, latest_run="waypoint_rl_eval_2026-03-02_21-36-31"
