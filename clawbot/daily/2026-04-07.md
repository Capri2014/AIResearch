## Pipeline PR #3 (10:30am PT)

**Theme:** Augmented SSL Training with Quality-Aware Sampling — connects quality metrics to SSL training.

### Slice: Augmented SSL Training

- **Created:** `training/pretrain/train_augmented_ssl.py`
  - `QualityAwareEpisodeDataset`: Dataset for augmented episodes with quality filtering
    - Filters by quality score (--quality-filter --min-quality)
    - Filters by difficulty (--difficulty easy|medium|hard)
    - On-the-fly image augmentations (brightness, contrast, saturation)
    - Fallback to placeholder images if images not found
  - `TinyMultiCamEncoder`: per-camera encoder with fusion
  - `multi_camera_simclr_loss`: contrastive loss treating cameras as positive pairs
  - CLI: --quality-filter, --min-quality, --difficulty, --no-augment, --brightness, --contrast, --saturation

- **Test results (20 steps, batch=4):**
  - Loss: 2.847 → 2.770 (converged)
  - Loaded 22 episodes after quality filtering
  - Total samples: 4,160
  - Output: out/augmented_ssl_test/encoder_final.pt
